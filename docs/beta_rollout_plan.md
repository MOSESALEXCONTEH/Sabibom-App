# Beta Rollout Plan

## Stage 1 — Internal
Testers: developer, trusted family/staff, one technical tester.  
Exit: no open P0; P1 primary workflows resolved or explicitly deferred with workaround.

## Stage 2 — Closed business beta (10–20)
Barbers, salons, retail, tailor, restaurant, service.  
Real daily use; report calculation mismatches immediately.

## Stage 3 — Wider
Only after Stage 2 stability. Manual promotion only.

## Feedback questions
1–15 as listed in Phase 12 brief (setup ease, sale ease, receipt, totals, stock, Sabi, confusion, speed, crash, most useful, missing, daily use, recommend, device).

Do not collect unnecessary sensitive data.
