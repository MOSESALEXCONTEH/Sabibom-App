# How to explain SabiBom (owner sheet)

Use this when talking to shop owners, staff, partners, or reviewers.

---

## One-page pitch

### 30 seconds
SabiBom is a shop app for recording sales and receipts, tracking stock, customers and credit, expenses, suppliers, and team access — with Sabi, an assistant that prepares records from what you say. You always review before anything is saved.

### 2 minutes
Most small shops keep sales in notebooks, WhatsApp, or memory. That makes stock wrong, credit hard to track, and end-of-day confusing.

SabiBom puts the shop in one place:
- Sell and share receipts
- Know what is in stock (and what is expiring)
- Track who owes you and who you owe
- Record expenses
- Give staff roles so cashiers don’t see everything
- See clear reports and close the day with End of Day

Sabi helps by voice or chat — for example “sell 2 rice bags to Aminata” — but it only prepares a draft. The merchant confirms. Sabi does not invent money totals or save by itself.

### Who it is for
Small retail shops and teams that need day-to-day shop operations — not full accounting software like QuickBooks.

### Who it is not for (yet)
Businesses that need advanced multi-branch accounting, full offline-first guarantees, or bank-grade compliance claims.

## Words to use (and avoid)

| Say this | Avoid this |
|----------|------------|
| Shop / business operations app | “AI will run your business for you” |
| Sales and receipts | Guaranteed profit |
| Stock / inventory | Perfect accounting |
| Customer credit / balance | Error-free AI |
| Sabi prepares drafts; you confirm | Bank-grade security (unless verified) |
| Roles and permissions | Features that are incomplete |

---

## Objection answers

**“Will AI mess up my money?”**  
No. Sabi prepares drafts. You confirm before anything is saved. It does not invent financial totals.

**“Can my cashier see profit?”**  
Only if you give that permission. Owners control roles.

**“Is Demo Business real money?”**  
No. Demo is practice data, separate from your real business.

**“Is this accounting software?”**  
It is a shop operations app: sales, stock, credit, expenses, team, reports. Not full legal accounting.

**“What about my data?”**  
Each business is behind a signed-in account. Staff only see what their role allows. Use Help / Feedback for support and deletion requests (see Privacy Policy).

**“Does everything work offline?”**  
Be honest about current limits. Do not promise full offline until it is solid.

# 7-minute live demo script

**Prep:** Use **Demo Business** only. Phone charged. Notifications quiet. Know where Sabi is (FAB / assistant).

| Min | Show | Say |
|-----|------|-----|
| 0:00–0:30 | Home / Dashboard | “This is your shop overview — today’s activity and what needs attention.” |
| 0:30–2:00 | Sales → New sale → Checkout → Receipt | “This is the till: add products, take payment, share or print a receipt.” |
| 2:00–3:00 | Products → one product / stock | “Stock lives here. Tracked products go down when you sell. You can also watch expiry.” |
| 3:00–4:00 | Customers → balance / payment | “If someone buys on credit, the balance shows here. Record a payment when they settle.” |
| 4:00–4:45 | Expenses or Suppliers (pick one) | “Money out: expenses, or purchases from suppliers that update stock.” |
| 4:45–5:30 | Team / roles (or explain verbally) | “Invite staff. Cashiers sell; profit and sensitive actions stay with managers/owners.” |
| 5:30–6:30 | Sabi (chat or voice) → draft → confirm | “Ask Sabi to prepare a sale or answer ‘who owes me?’ Then you review and confirm.” |
| 6:30–7:00 | Reports or End of Day | “Reports show verified numbers. End of Day helps match cash in the drawer.” |

### Optional 30-second closer
“Sales, stock, credit, team, reports — one app. Sabi speeds the work; you stay in control.”
