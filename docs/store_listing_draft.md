## Full description

SabiBom helps small businesses keep daily operations organized from an Android phone.

Record sales and create clear receipts. Track products, branch stock and purchases. Manage customers, suppliers, expenses and balances. Review dashboards and business reports for the selected branch or, when authorized, across the whole business.

Sabi, the built-in business assistant, helps users understand their records and prepare supported actions. Users review information before it is saved.

Key features:

- Sales history and receipts
- Products, services and branch inventory
- Purchases, suppliers and expenses
- Customers, clients and balances
- Branch-aware dashboards and reports
- Staff roles, permissions and branch access
- Low-stock, expiry and operational alerts
- Sabi business assistant with voice input
- Dark mode and configurable receipt designs

SabiBom supports both product-selling and service businesses. Available features depend on business type, permissions and plan.

SabiBom is a business operations tool, not a bank, accounting firm, tax adviser or legal adviser. Review important records and professional decisions independently.
